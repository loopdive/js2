## Findings

1. **IR-claimed on all four lanes (compile-once): 01, 04, 11 only.** 04 still carries host-lane-only imports `env.__call_function_1..4`/`__box_number`/`__unbox_number` (0 imports in strict/standalone/wasi) — the closure is IR (`closure.new`/`closure.call`, lower.ts:2349/2390) but the host lane also registers the legacy unmatched-callee dispatcher (`hostCallableFallbackTerminal`, closure-exports.ts:1264-1285, suppressed when `ctx.standalone || ctx.wasi || native-first`).
2. **Lane-dependent imports are ALL in `env` and host-lane-only.** Callable-specific ones: `__call_function` (array ABI), `__call_function_0..4` (fixed arity), `__bind_function` (the only one on the dual-mode allowlist, host-import-allowlist.ts:332), `__make_callback` (the sole callable capability record in the R6 catalogue: `async.callback.wrap`, runtime-host-capabilities.ts:375), `__register_fnctor_instance` (also present on the strict gc lane for shape 10). Strict/standalone/wasi lanes are import-free for every shape except 09/09b (standalone leaks `EventTarget_addEventListener`/`Element_set_textContent` as #2961 warnings; wasi hard-fails "DOM global 'EventTarget' is not available in WASI target").
3. **Legacy fallbacks and reasons** — `body-shape-rejected` ×6 (02 fn-typed local annotation, select.ts:5723; 03/05 closure return-type annotation missing or non-scalar, :6010; 06/12 function identifier used as a value, :9264; 10 `new (expr)` non-identifier callee, :9047), `function-invocation-method-unsupported` (07, :9792), `array-method-unsupported` (08, :9769 — only `push` and holey `filter` admitted), `call-resolution-unsupported` (13, :5881 nested self-reference), `call-graph-closure` (05 entry; 12 `compose` on non-host lanes, :1153).
4. **Compile-twice is the dominant family-3 hazard, not selector rejection**: `scale`, `sum3`, `Point`, `compose` (gc-host) and `install` (09/09b gc-host) are IR-emitted AND legacy-emitted. Cause: the compile-once skip allowlist (`computeIrFirstSkipUnitIds`, ir-overlay-safety.ts:328-404) admits only number/bool/string signatures via `positionDomain` (:368) — a `this:` object param, a function-typed param/return or an `EventTarget` param yields `null`, and `irFirstBodyIsProvenLowerable` (ir-first-gate.ts:96-101) explicitly keeps closure/extern/`new` shapes COMPILE-TWICE.
5. **Strict gc lane (`strictNoHostImports:true`) is a native-strings regime, not host mode minus imports**: `nativeStringsRequiredByPolicy = standalone || wasi || strictEnvImportGate || native-first` (target-profile.ts:124-125; `strictEnvImportGate = input.strictNoHostImports ?? target === "wasi"`, :80), hence 22 KB for a two-function module, and it is refused for the exact invocation lane (codegen/index.ts:4292-4300, "a distinct and still-unproven regime"). Allowlist violations on it are `severity:"warning"` with `success:true` (05, 09, 12).
6. **R6 vocabulary for family 3 is empty except `async.callback.wrap`**: no callable `IntrinsicId` (intrinsics.ts:95-103 — numeric/number/boolean/extern/math only), no `RuntimeManifestPolicy` field for calls/closures/bind (runtime-manifest.ts policy fields are number/boolean/extern/generator/string only), 89 raw `ctx.nativeStrings|standalone|wasi` reads in integration.ts, only two on callable paths (:7268, :7314).
